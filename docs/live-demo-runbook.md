# [SPATIALTWIN] // Live Demo Runbook

Duration: **6 minutes**.  
Setup: Left display — SpatialTwin in browser (`http://localhost:3000`). Right display — Google Sheets, Telegram, and Claude Desktop / MCP client.

---

## [01] MINUTE-BY-MINUTE TIMELINE

```
[0:00 - 1:00] Intro: Static BI Reports vs Live 2.5D Spatial Digital Twin
[1:00 - 2:00] Stage 1: Google Sheets -> Warm Amber Bed Glow & 59:59 Countdown
[2:00 - 3:00] Stage 2: Guest Wait Delay & Irritation Smoke (SLA Alert)
[3:00 - 4:00] Stage 3: Telegram Payment -> Gold Coin Fountain from Cash Chest
[4:00 - 4:45] Stage 4: Staff Burnout Alert (Stamina Drop < 20%)
[4:45 - 5:20] Stage 5: 5-Star Client Review (Sakura Petal Bloom)
[5:20 - 6:00] Finale: Live Salon Remodel via AI MCP Agent
```

---

## [02] SPEAKER SCRIPT & STAGE ACTIONS

### [0:00 - 1:00] Introduction
* **Narrative:** *"Small business owners and receptionists look at Excel, CRM tables, and static dashboards for barely 3 minutes a week. Spreadsheets don't show that a guest has been waiting 20 minutes in the lobby or that a therapist is exhausted after five consecutive sessions. SpatialTwin turns dry operational telemetry into a living 2.5D spatial twin that stays on the reception display for hours."*

### [1:00 - 2:00] Stage 1: Google Sheets -> Session Start
* **Action:** Click **`[1] Session (Sheets)`** in the bottom OpenCode TUI prompt bar (or change cell `B2` in Google Sheets to `Session 60m`).
* **Visual Output:** Tatami Bed #1 (Sakura) illuminates with a warm `#FFAA33` additive glow and displays the `[+] SESSION · 59:59` countdown badge.
* **Narrative:** *"The receptionist logs a booking in a standard spreadsheet — within milliseconds, the treatment room comes alive on the diorama."*

### [2:00 - 3:00] Stage 2: Guest Wait Delay (SLA Breach)
* **Action:** Click **`[2] Delay (SLA)`**.
* **Visual Output:** Irritation smoke rises above Guest Kenji in the waiting area while a red warning pulse highlights the SLA violation.
* **Narrative:** *"When a guest waits past the 15-minute SLA threshold, the bottleneck is immediately visible in physical space."*

### [3:00 - 4:00] Stage 3: Payment Received (`/pay`)
* **Action:** Click **`[3] Payment (/pay)`** (or send `/pay 4500` in the Telegram bot).
* **Visual Output:** The Wooden Cash Chest bounces and erupts with a fountain of gold coins across the reception floor.
* **Narrative:** *"Every transaction in the POS or CRM provides instant visual feedback to the floor team."*

### [4:00 - 4:45] Stage 4: Staff Burnout (Stamina Engine)
* **Action:** Click **`[4] Burnout (Stamina)`**.
* **Visual Output:** Therapist Somchai's stamina bar drops to `14%` (danger red `#ff3b30`), and a `[!] BREAK NEEDED` badge appears above him.
* **Narrative:** *"The stamina engine continuously models therapist fatigue based on treatment intensity, preventing staff burnout before service quality drops."*

### [4:45 - 5:20] Stage 5: 5-Star Client Review
* **Action:** Click **`[5] 5-Star Review`**.
* **Visual Output:** Swirling pink sakura petals cascade across the entire 2.5D diorama.
* **Narrative:** *"A new 5-star review celebrates the team's work across the entire floor."*

### [5:20 - 6:00] Finale: AI MCP Spatial Remodel
* **Action:** Click **`[6] AI MCP Remodel`** (or prompt Claude Desktop via MCP: *"Replace the right lounge sofa with a VIP Hinoki Cypress Onsen and bind it to cell Sheet1!F2"*).
* **Visual Output:** Without reloading the page, the sofa despawns and a steaming VIP Hinoki Cypress Onsen appears in its place, bound to `Sheet1!F2`.
* **Narrative:** *"SpatialTwin is natively open to AI agents via the Model Context Protocol — operators can reconfigure both physical layout and telemetry bindings using natural language."*
