Modern AI agents (Claude, Cursor, custom assistants) query SQL tables with ease, but lack a physical spatial canvas to comprehend, test, and rearrange real physical environments. They cannot detect bottlenecks or spatial layout friction.

In actual hospitality and retail, operational parameters change daily. Wait tolerances shift, therapist break intervals vary, and seasonal room usage fluctuates. Hardcoded software requires hiring developers for tiny operational adjustments.

12 Declarative Tools for AI Agents

SpatialTwin treats spatial geometry and business rules as mutable state. AI assistants read live layouts, spawn equipment, bind real-time streams, and reconfigure operational constraints via natural language.

  • get_layout(facility_id) Returns coordinates, active stations, staff locations, and waiting clients.
  • place_station(entity_type, grid_x, grid_y, orientation) Spawns interactive massage tables, onsen tubs, or tea stations on the 2.5D grid.
  • bind_data_stream(entity_id, source_url, json_path) Connects POS or Google Sheet cells to live ambient lights and status bubbles.
  • configure_business_rules(rule_id, operator, threshold, alert) Dynamically updates wait-time tolerances and staff rest requirements without re-deploying code.
